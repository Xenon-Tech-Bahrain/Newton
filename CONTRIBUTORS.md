## Attribution in history

Commits written by the OpenCode agent carry a `Co-authored-by` trailer, so
`git log` records both the direction behind a change and the agent that typed it:

```
Co-authored-by: OpenCode <noreply@opencode.ai>
```

The other agents are credited here rather than in commit trailers, because their
vendors do not publish a noreply address to attribute them with.
